Why these stories matter

A participant is more than a success story.

At CoWiTa, the young people we work with are not simply examples of what an organisation has achieved. They are people with their own abilities, ambitions, decisions, challenges and voices.

Participant stories will help show what happens when talent is given room to grow — from the first moment someone recognises an ability in themselves to the moments when that ability begins creating new possibilities.

This page will grow alongside CoWiTa's programmes. For now, the stories below represent the kinds of journeys we intend to document as the work develops.

What we will document

Not just where someone ends up.

Growth is rarely one dramatic moment. We want to document the smaller decisions, relationships and milestones that make a creative journey meaningful.

01 / THE BEGINNING

The first spark

What did they discover about themselves? What made them realise that a talent they enjoyed might be something worth developing?

02 / THE WORK

The process of getting better

Practice, feedback, discipline, experimentation and the people who help turn natural ability into a stronger skill.

03 / THE CREATION

Making something real

The work itself — a performance, song, drawing, written piece, production, project or another expression of the participant's ability.

04 / THE OPPORTUNITY

What happens next

New experiences, collaborations, platforms, income possibilities or other opportunities that emerge as participants continue developing their abilities.

The CoWiTa journey

From potential to possibility.

CoWiTa's model connects talent development with the wider journey around it. Participant stories will help make that journey visible — while recognising that every person's path will be different.

01

Identify

Recognising an ability, interest or creative potential.

02

Develop

Building skill through practice, learning and mentorship.

03

Create

Turning developing skills into real creative work.

04

Platform

Creating spaces where young people can share their work.

05

Opportunity

Connecting ability and creative work with possibilities.

06

Sustain

Supporting pathways toward continued growth and impact.

This is CoWiTa's intended development model, not a promise that every participant will move through every stage in the same way. Individual journeys will remain personal and different.

Our storytelling promise

Their story should still feel like theirs.

People before promotion.

Stories should help people understand the person, not turn a participant into a marketing statistic.

Voice matters.

Wherever possible, participants should have a meaningful role in how their experiences are represented.

Strength, not spectacle.

We will focus on ability, agency, growth and possibility rather than using hardship as the centre of someone's story.

Consent is part of the story.

Personal stories, images and experiences should only be shared through an appropriate, respectful process.

Evidence over exaggeration.

When a story includes an outcome, CoWiTa will distinguish what is known, what is experienced and what is still being developed.
