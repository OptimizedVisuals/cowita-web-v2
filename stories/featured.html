Why stories matter

Impact is more than a number.

Behind every programme is a person with a different starting point, a different ability and a different possibility.

CoWiTa's stories will make those journeys visible. They will show what happens when talent is recognised, when someone receives the right guidance, and when creative ability is given room to develop.

This section will eventually become a living record of the people and communities connected to CoWiTa.
