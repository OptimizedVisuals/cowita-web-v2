Why this space exists

The work is a journey too.

CoWiTa is being built around a simple belief: talent grows when people have the opportunity, guidance and space to develop it.

Our news and updates section will document what that looks like in practice — from programme developments and creative activities to partnerships, opportunities, community moments and lessons from the work.

We will also use this space to share ideas and conversations relevant to young creatives, mentors, partners and communities.
