I Have Talent
Our Impact

We measure the change talent can create.

CoWiTa is not only interested in discovering talent. We want to understand what happens when that talent is developed, supported, connected to opportunity and given room to grow.

Talent → opportunity → impact. Our impact framework follows the journey, not just the activity.
What impact means

Activity is not the same as change.

For CoWiTa, impact means a young person's capacity, confidence and opportunities are meaningfully changed.

A workshop matters because of what it enables afterwards. A mentorship matters because it can strengthen a skill, open a relationship or change the direction of a creative journey.

That means we will not measure success only by how many events we hold or how many people attend them.

We want to understand whether young people are developing stronger skills, creating meaningful work, accessing opportunities and moving closer to sustainable livelihoods.

Our theory of change

The journey from potential to possibility.

CoWiTa's model is built around a simple progression: discover potential, develop ability, create real work, connect that work to opportunity and support continued growth.

01 / POTENTIAL

Talent is identified.

Young people are recognised for abilities they may already be practising in their communities, schools or everyday lives.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

Ability becomes skill.

Training, practice and mentorship strengthen creative ability and build practical knowledge.

03 / CREATION

Skill becomes work.

Participants create performances, projects, content and other outputs that demonstrate what they can do.

04 / OPPORTUNITY

Work meets opportunity.

Creative output can be connected to audiences, collaborations, commissions, platforms and markets.

05 / IMPACT

Opportunity creates change.

Stronger skills, confidence, networks and income potential can contribute to longer-term livelihoods and community development.

Areas of impact

What we hope to change.

CoWiTa's impact is broader than artistic ability. Creative development can influence confidence, skills, relationships, opportunity and livelihoods.

01

Creative Skills

Young people develop stronger technical and creative abilities in the disciplines they choose to pursue.

02

Confidence & Agency

Participants gain confidence to express themselves, make decisions, create and take ownership of their development.

03

Opportunity

Young creatives gain access to platforms, networks, collaborations and practical opportunities to use their abilities.

04

Livelihood Potential

Creative skills can become connected to income, commissions, employment, entrepreneurship and other sustainable pathways.

05

Networks & Mentorship

Participants build relationships with mentors, peers, practitioners and organisations within the creative ecosystem.

06

Community Impact

Creative activity can contribute to stronger communities by creating spaces for expression, collaboration and shared opportunity.

Measuring the journey

Numbers will come later. The framework starts now.

CoWiTa is still building its programmes and systems. We will not manufacture impact statistics before the work exists. Instead, we are establishing the measures that future programmes will report against.

Reach
Coming soon

Young people reached through programmes, workshops and community activities.

Development
Coming soon

Participants completing training, mentorship and structured development pathways.

Opportunity
Coming soon

Collaborations, performances, commissions, placements and other opportunities created.

Livelihoods
Coming soon

Participants progressing toward income generation and sustainable creative livelihoods.

Evidence matters

We want to show the work, not simply claim the impact.

As CoWiTa grows, participant stories, programme records, outcome data and feedback will become part of how we understand what is working.

This will allow us to improve programmes, communicate honestly with partners and supporters, and understand where CoWiTa is actually creating value.

Our commitment

Measure what matters.

We will distinguish between what CoWiTa has done, what participants experienced and what changed as a result. Impact reporting will develop alongside the programmes themselves.

We want a future where creative talent is not left as potential.

The change CoWiTa seeks is simple: young people should be able to recognise their abilities, develop them, find meaningful opportunities to use them and build lives in which their talents have real value.
