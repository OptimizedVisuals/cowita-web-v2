The Change We Seek

A future where talent has a pathway.

We imagine a future where a child who can dance, a young person who writes, an emerging musician, artist or performer does not have to leave their talent behind simply because they cannot see where it can take them.

Our long-term ambition Talent becomes skill. Skill becomes opportunity. Opportunity becomes impact.

The Future We Are Working Toward

Potential should not be limited by the opportunities around it.

Across communities, young people are already creating, performing, imagining and building. The challenge is that talent alone does not automatically become a sustainable pathway.

A young person may have the ability to dance, sing, draw, write or act, but still lack the mentorship, skills, confidence, exposure, networks and opportunities needed to develop that ability further.

CoWiTa exists to help change that journey. We want to make the distance between “I am good at this” and “I can build something with this” smaller.

Our belief is simple.

Talent deserves development, development deserves opportunity, and opportunity should create a pathway toward purpose, sustainable livelihoods and contribution to community.

What Change Looks Like

We are not only trying to create better creatives. We are trying to create better possibilities.

Our intended change reaches beyond a single workshop or creative project. It is about what becomes possible when a young person's ability is recognized, developed and connected to the right opportunities.

01

Capability

Young people develop practical creative, professional and entrepreneurial skills that strengthen what they already know how to do.

02

Confidence & Agency

Young people begin to see their talent as something they can actively develop, communicate, use and build a future around.

03

Visibility

Creative work gets opportunities to be seen, shared and connected with audiences, collaborators and people who can open new doors.

04

Opportunity

Talent is connected to practical pathways such as commissions, collaborations, further learning, platforms, entrepreneurship and future work.

05

Sustainable Pathways

Over time, creative ability can become part of a young person's livelihood, enterprise or long-term professional journey.

06

Community Impact

Young people use their developed skills and creativity to contribute to their families, communities and the wider creative ecosystem.

The CoWiTa Pathway

Change happens through a journey, not a single intervention.

Our approach is built around a progression from discovering potential to creating sustainable value from it.

01

Identify

Recognize talent, interests and potential that may already exist within a young person.

02

Develop

Build technical, creative, professional and personal capabilities around that talent.

03

Create

Turn learning into practice through projects, performances, creative work and experimentation.

04

Platform

Create opportunities for young creatives to be seen, heard, connected and taken seriously.

05

Monetize

Help connect developed skills to opportunities for income, enterprise, commissions and sustainable work.

06

Sustain

Support longer-term growth so creative ability can continue creating value beyond the initial programme.

Beyond the Individual

When one young person grows, the impact can move outward.

The change we seek is not limited to individual talent. Stronger young creatives can become collaborators, mentors, entrepreneurs, role models and contributors within their communities.

01

Families

Young people's abilities can become more visible and understandable as legitimate pathways for growth.

02

Communities

Creative activity can strengthen connection, expression, participation and local problem-solving.

03

Mentors & Partners

Experienced people and organizations can become part of a stronger pipeline for developing emerging talent.

04

The Creative Ecosystem

More prepared young creatives can contribute to a healthier, more connected and more economically active creative sector.

Our Commitment

We want to measure change, not just activity.

A workshop delivered is an activity. A participant attending is participation. Neither automatically means lasting impact.

As CoWiTa grows, we intend to build systems that help us understand what happens after participation: what young people learn, what they create, what opportunities they access and how their journeys develop over time.

Our detailed impact measurement framework is Coming Soon as we establish the systems required to collect, verify and report meaningful data responsibly.

What We Will Look For

“Did the opportunity create a meaningful next step?”

We will increasingly look beyond attendance and activity counts to understand progression in skills, confidence, creative output, mentorship, visibility, opportunity and sustainable pathways.

The future we want to see starts with what we choose to build now.
