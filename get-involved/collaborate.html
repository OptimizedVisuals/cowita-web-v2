Have an idea? Let's build it together.

CoWiTa welcomes people, creatives, organisations and communities who want to turn ideas into meaningful projects, experiences and opportunities for young people.

Explore Collaboration Start a Conversation
Ideas become stronger together. Collaboration allows different skills, perspectives and resources to create something that none of us could build alone.
Collaboration at CoWiTa

Not every idea needs a programme. Sometimes it needs a partner.

We believe some of the most meaningful opportunities begin with a simple idea and the willingness to build it together.

You might have an idea for a workshop, creative project, performance, campaign, community event, production or learning experience. You may have a skill or resource that could make an existing idea stronger.

Collaboration is about bringing those possibilities together around a clear purpose: creating meaningful experiences and opportunities for children and young people.

What we can build

Collaboration can start almost anywhere.

These are examples of the kinds of ideas that could connect with CoWiTa's work. They are possibilities, not existing programmes or promises of delivery.

01

Creative projects

Develop a creative project where young people can learn, create, participate and produce work together.

02

Workshops

Design practical sessions around dance, music, drawing, writing, acting, production or other creative and professional skills.

03

Events

Create showcases, community events, exhibitions, performances or experiences that give young people a platform.

04

Content & media

Produce stories, films, campaigns, interviews, digital content and other creative work that amplifies young voices.

05

Community initiatives

Work with communities to create creative activities that bring young people together around talent, learning and participation.

06

New ideas

If your idea does not fit a category, that is fine. We are interested in exploring thoughtful ideas that align with CoWiTa's purpose.

Imagine the possibilities

Collaboration should create something useful.

Creative development

A young dance crew creates its first production.

A choreographer, videographer and community partner could work with emerging dancers to develop, document and showcase an original performance.

Skills & opportunity

Young creatives learn how to turn skill into work.

Creative professionals could collaborate with CoWiTa to design a practical workshop connecting talent, production skills, professional practice and real-world opportunities.

Community

A local creative space becomes a place to grow.

A community organisation with space could work with CoWiTa to create recurring creative activities for children and young people.

Storytelling

Young voices tell their own stories.

Creatives, media professionals and young participants could collaborate on documentary, photography, writing or digital storytelling projects.

A simple process

Start with the idea. Build from there.

As CoWiTa's collaboration system develops, the process will remain intentionally simple.

01 — Share

Bring the idea.

Tell us what you are thinking, who it is for and what you would like to create.

02 — Explore

Find the connection.

We explore how the idea connects with CoWiTa's purpose, creative work and young people.

03 — Build

Shape the project.

Define the people, resources, responsibilities, timeline and outcomes needed to make the idea work.

04 — Create

Make it happen.

Work together to deliver the project, learn from the process and create something meaningful.

What makes a good collaboration

The project matters. So does the way we build it.

CoWiTa is interested in collaborations that put meaningful participation and development at the centre.

A strong collaboration should create value for the young people involved, the communities around them and the people bringing their expertise to the project.

01
Purpose first

The project should have a clear reason for existing and a meaningful outcome.

02
Young people at the centre

Young participants should be active contributors, not simply an audience for the project.

03
Learn while creating

Wherever possible, collaboration should build practical skills alongside the creative output.

04
Shared responsibility

Everyone should understand what they are bringing and what they are responsible for.

Collaboration system

Future versions of CoWiTa will make it easier to submit project ideas, discover collaboration opportunities, find creative contributors and track collaborative initiatives.
