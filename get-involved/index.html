I Have Talent
Get involved

There is a place for you here.

CoWiTa is built around people. Whether you carry a creative talent, want to develop a skill, have knowledge to share, or can help create opportunities, your contribution can become part of the journey.

Find your pathway Talk to CoWiTa
Talent grows through connection. CoWiTa brings young people, mentors, collaborators and partners into one ecosystem.
More than participation

The ecosystem works when people move together.

CoWiTa is not only a programme young people attend. It is a network of people who can contribute different forms of knowledge, creativity, opportunity and support.

A young dancer may need mentorship. A writer may need a platform. A mentor may need a place to share years of experience. A business may have an opportunity that a young creative has been looking for.

Our role is to create the connections that allow these pieces to meet.

Choose the pathway that best describes where you are right now. You can always take another pathway later.

The CoWiTa ecosystem

Different roles. One direction.

CoWiTa's model depends on different people contributing different forms of value. The young person brings potential. The mentor brings experience. The partner can bring access, resources or opportunity.

When those contributions connect, talent has a stronger chance of becoming sustainable work and meaningful community impact.

01
Young people Bring talent, curiosity, creativity and the desire to grow.
02
Mentors Share knowledge, experience and practical guidance.
03
Collaborators Build projects, creative work and shared experiences.
04
Partners Help create resources, platforms and pathways to opportunity.

Not every contribution has to begin with a programme.

Sometimes the most valuable first step is simply connecting with CoWiTa and understanding where your support can make the greatest difference.
