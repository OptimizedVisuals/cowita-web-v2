Learning at CoWiTa

You don't have to start with everything figured out.

Sometimes the first step is simply having someone recognise what you can do and help you develop it.

CoWiTa is designed around that idea. Learning should not only mean collecting information. It should help a young person become better at something, understand how that ability can be used and gain the confidence to keep developing it.

Through creative development, practical skills, mentorship and opportunities to create, CoWiTa aims to build pathways where learning can lead somewhere.

Some of these programmes are still being developed. As CoWiTa grows, this page will become the entry point for learners to discover available programmes, workshops and learning opportunities.

Our learning philosophy

Learning should move with you.

01

Discover

Identify interests, natural strengths and areas where the learner wants to grow.

02

Practise

Build ability through repetition, experimentation, feedback and practical exercises.

03

Create

Apply learning by making something real, performing, publishing or contributing to a project.

04

Progress

Continue developing through mentorship, new challenges and future opportunities.

More than a classroom

Learning becomes stronger when someone walks with you.

CoWiTa sees mentorship as an important part of development. A learner should be able to receive guidance from people who have experience in the creative or professional area they are exploring.

The future CoWiTa platform will make it easier to connect learners with mentors, practitioners and other people who can contribute to their growth.

01
Guidance Learn from people with relevant experience.
02
Feedback Understand what is working and where to improve.
03
Exposure Connect learning with real people, projects and creative environments.
