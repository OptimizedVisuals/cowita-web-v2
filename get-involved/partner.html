Partner With CoWiTa

Help create pathways for talent.

CoWiTa believes that transforming talent requires more than individual effort. It requires people, organisations and institutions willing to create opportunities together.

Explore Partnership Contact CoWiTa
Why partnerships matter

Talent grows faster when the right people build the pathway together.

CoWiTa is designed to connect young people's creative potential with the people, resources, knowledge and opportunities that can help that potential grow.

01

Access

Help young creatives access spaces, equipment, platforms, networks and experiences that may otherwise be difficult to reach.

02

Knowledge

Share professional knowledge, technical expertise, industry experience and practical guidance with emerging talent.

03

Opportunity

Create pathways through commissions, performances, exhibitions, collaborations, placements and other meaningful opportunities.

04

Sustainability

Help build systems that allow creative development to continue beyond a single programme, event or training session.

Who we partner with

Different strengths. One shared purpose.

CoWiTa's partnership model is intentionally broad. We are interested in relationships that help young people move from potential to practical opportunity.

A partner does not necessarily need to provide financial support. Expertise, access, equipment, spaces, networks, platforms and opportunities can all contribute to the journey.

01
Schools & educational institutions

Identify talent, provide learning environments and connect young people to development pathways.

02
Creative professionals & businesses

Share expertise, industry exposure, mentorship and opportunities for practical experience.

03
Community organisations

Help identify local talent and create spaces where young people can develop and participate.

04
Companies & brands

Support programmes, commission creative work and help create real-world opportunities.

05
Foundations & development organisations

Collaborate on initiatives focused on youth development, skills and creative livelihoods.

06
Public institutions

Connect CoWiTa's work with wider youth, education, culture and creative-economy efforts.

Partnership possibilities

Partnership can take many forms.

We do not want to force every organisation into the same partnership model. The right structure depends on what each partner can contribute and what young people need.

01

Programme partnerships

Co-design or support programmes that develop creative skills, confidence and practical experience.

02

Opportunity partnerships

Open pathways for young creatives through performances, commissions, internships, exhibitions and projects.

03

Knowledge partnerships

Provide specialist training, mentorship, professional talks and technical expertise.

04

Resource partnerships

Contribute equipment, venues, technology, production resources or other practical support.

05

Visibility partnerships

Help emerging creatives reach audiences through media, events, platforms and communications networks.

06

Strategic partnerships

Build longer-term relationships around youth development, creative livelihoods and community impact.

The value of partnership

Bring what you can. Help build what is missing.

Partnerships do not have to begin with a large commitment. Sometimes the most valuable contribution is an introduction, a few hours of expertise, access to a workspace or an opportunity for a young creative to demonstrate what they can do.

CoWiTa's role is to connect those contributions into a meaningful development pathway.

✓ Creative spaces and equipment

How it could work

From conversation to collaboration.

As CoWiTa develops its partnership infrastructure, relationships will follow a simple process.

01

Connect

Tell us who you are, what you do and what interests you about CoWiTa.

02

Explore

We identify areas where your capabilities and CoWiTa's work could meaningfully intersect.

03

Design

Together we define the scope, responsibilities and intended outcomes.

04

Build

The partnership moves into implementation, learning and practical delivery.

Partnership portal

In a future version of the CoWiTa platform, partners will be able to explore partnership opportunities, submit partnership proposals, discover programmes and manage ongoing collaborations through a dedicated digital experience.
