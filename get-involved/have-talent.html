I Have Talent

I HAVE TALENT

You already have
something.

Maybe you dance. Maybe you draw, write, act or make music. Maybe you are still figuring out exactly what your thing is. CoWiTa starts there — with what you can already do, and the potential to take it further.

Tell Us About Your Talent Explore Our Work

START WHERE YOU ARE

Your talent does not have to be perfect to matter.

A talent can begin as something you do in your free time, something your friends keep asking you to do, or something you have never really considered a talent at all.

What matters is that there is something worth developing. CoWiTa is built around the idea that young people should have opportunities to identify their strengths, build their abilities and discover where those abilities can take them.

You do not need to arrive with a finished portfolio, professional experience or a clear career plan. You need curiosity, commitment and a willingness to grow.

WHAT DEVELOPMENT CAN LOOK LIKE

Talent is only the beginning.

CoWiTa's approach is not simply about finding what someone is good at. It is about creating the conditions for that ability to grow into something useful, purposeful and sustainable.

01

Identify

Recognise the ability, interest or creative instinct that is already there — even when it is still rough.

02

Develop

Build technique, discipline, confidence and practical skills through learning, practice and mentorship.

03

Create

Put your growing ability into practice by making work, performing, experimenting and solving real problems.
