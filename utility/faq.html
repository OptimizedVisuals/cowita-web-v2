ABOUT COWITA

Understanding what we do

CoWiTa is a youth-focused initiative that helps children and young people identify, develop and strengthen their talents into practical skills, opportunities and pathways for growth.

Our work connects creative development, skills, mentorship, platforms and opportunity.

CoWiTa is the name of the initiative and its community. The identity is built around collaboration, creativity, development and turning talent into meaningful opportunity.

CoWiTa starts with the person rather than a predefined path. We look at what a young person is already good at, interested in or capable of developing, then work toward the skills, experience and opportunities that can help that potential grow.

No. CoWiTa is not intended to replace formal education. It is designed to complement learning by creating additional pathways for creativity, skills, mentorship, experience and opportunity.

YOUNG PEOPLE

For young people and families

CoWiTa is focused on children and young people, while also working with mentors, educators, creatives, organisations, families, communities and partners who can contribute to their development.

No. You do not need to be an expert. You might already have a clear talent, be discovering an interest, or simply want to learn. CoWiTa is interested in potential as well as developed ability.

Current creative pathways include dance, music, drawing, writing and acting. CoWiTa also supports practical skills development and mentorship around those creative and personal growth pathways.

More areas can be added as the programme and network grow.

Simply expressing your interest does not require a payment. Specific programmes or activities may have their own arrangements when they are introduced, and those details will be communicated clearly.

GETTING INVOLVED

Ways to become part of CoWiTa

There are several pathways: you can bring your talent, learn a skill, become a mentor, collaborate on a project, partner with CoWiTa, or support the work.

Visit the Get Involved page to find the route that fits you.

Yes. People with useful experience, knowledge, professional skills or life perspective can express interest in mentoring.

Mentorship structures are still being developed, so participation will depend on the needs and programmes available at the time.

Yes. CoWiTa welcomes conversations with schools, creative organisations, businesses, community organisations, development organisations and other groups interested in creating meaningful experiences and opportunities for young people.

See Collaborate or Partner to explore the difference.

Collaboration is generally about working together on a project, programme, workshop, production or experience. Partnership is broader and can involve longer-term strategic, financial, institutional, network or resource commitments.

FIND OPPORTUNITY

Understanding the opportunity directory

It is CoWiTa's growing space for discovering pathways to learn, gain experience, create, connect and eventually access work and other opportunities.

Visit Find Opportunity to see what is currently available and what is being developed.

No. The directory distinguishes between pathways that are currently available and opportunities marked "Coming Soon." We do not want to present an opportunity as open until the relevant details and pathway are actually ready.

The long-term directory may include creative opportunities, internships and work experience, training programmes, jobs and gigs, grants and funding, and opportunities created through CoWiTa's partner network.

The directory is being developed, so a full opportunity submission system is not available yet. Organisations interested in creating or sharing opportunities can start by contacting CoWiTa.

SUPPORT & PARTNERSHIPS

Supporting what CoWiTa is building

Support can take different forms. You can contribute financially, provide useful resources or expertise, create opportunities, make introductions, or build a longer-term partnership with CoWiTa.

Visit Support CoWiTa to explore the available pathways.

Absolutely. Expertise, equipment, spaces, networks, platforms, mentorship and access to opportunities can all contribute to the work.

In many cases, the right connection or resource can be just as valuable as a financial contribution.

CoWiTa is developing its support and contribution structures. If you are interested in supporting the work financially, please use the Financial Support page or contact us so we can discuss the appropriate next step.

Yes. CoWiTa is interested in relationships that can create meaningful value for young people and for the organisations involved.

Partnership conversations can cover resources, expertise, networks, funding, access, programmes and long-term collaboration.
