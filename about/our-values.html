I Have Talent
Our values

How we choose to show up.

CoWiTa is not only defined by what we do. It is also defined by how we work with young people, communities, mentors and partners along the way.

People first. Talent, dignity, growth and opportunity remain at the centre of the CoWiTa approach.
What matters to us

Our values shape the pathway.

We want the journey from talent to opportunity to be as meaningful as the outcome.

That means creating spaces where young people are respected, listened to and encouraged to grow. It means treating creative ability seriously while recognising that every person's journey will look different.

Our values therefore guide the decisions we make, the programmes we develop, the relationships we build and the opportunities we create.

They are not simply words on a page. They are the standard we want CoWiTa to hold itself to.

The principles behind CoWiTa

Six principles guide the work.

Together, these values describe the kind of organisation CoWiTa is trying to become—and the kind of environment we want young people to experience.

01
01

Potential

We look beyond where a young person is today. Talent can begin as something informal, unfinished or unnoticed. We believe development starts by recognising what is already there.

02
02

Growth

Talent becomes stronger through practice, learning, feedback and mentorship. We value progress over perfection and create room for people to develop at different stages of their journey.

03
03

Opportunity

Development should lead somewhere. We work toward connecting creative ability with platforms, audiences, networks, practical experience and pathways that can create meaningful opportunities.

04
04

Dignity

Every young person deserves to be treated with respect. We seek to create environments where people can express themselves, learn from mistakes and contribute without being reduced to their circumstances.

05
05

Community

Creative development does not happen in isolation. We believe in building relationships between young people, mentors, families, communities, organisations and the wider creative ecosystem.

06
06

Responsibility

Opportunity comes with responsibility. We aim to use creativity as a force for personal development, economic possibility and positive contribution to communities.

Values only matter when they become action.

These principles influence how CoWiTa intends to design programmes, work with participants and build relationships with the people and organisations around us.

01
We listen before we design. Programmes should respond to real people, real abilities and real community contexts.
02
We develop, not simply discover. Identifying talent is only the beginning. Development requires time, practice and guidance.
03
We create real pathways. Skills should connect to experiences, platforms, networks and opportunities wherever possible.
04
We build with others. Mentors, communities, organisations and partners all have a role in creating stronger pathways.
05
We measure progress honestly. As CoWiTa grows, we intend to learn from what works, what does not and what participants actually experience.
“Talent is the beginning. What we build around it determines where it can go.”
