OUR APPROACH

Talent is the beginning.

CoWiTa is designed to move young people from what they can already do toward the skills, confidence, opportunities and pathways that can help that ability become sustainable.

The CoWiTa principle Don't stop at discovering talent. Build the pathway around it.
01 / THE IDEA

A talent-first approach.

A young person may already know how to dance, sing, draw, write, act or create. That ability matters. But talent on its own does not automatically create opportunity.

CoWiTa starts with the ability that already exists and asks what needs to happen next: What skills need to be developed? Who can provide guidance? Where can the young person create real work? Who needs to see it? And how can that work become an opportunity that can be sustained?

This means CoWiTa is not built around talent discovery alone. Discovery is the starting point of a longer development pathway.

We do not want talent to be the end of the conversation. We want it to be the beginning of one.

02 / THE MODEL

From ability to sustainability.

CoWiTa's model connects the stages that are often treated separately. The goal is to create a clearer progression from natural ability to practical capability, visibility, opportunity and long-term sustainability.

01

Identify

Recognise creative ability and understand where a young person's potential, interests and strengths lie.

02

Develop

Turn raw ability into practical skills through learning, practice, guidance and mentorship.

03

Create

Give young creatives opportunities to make real work, experiment, build portfolios and learn through doing.

04

Platform

Help creative work become visible by connecting talent with audiences, communities, collaborators and relevant platforms.

05

Monetize

Connect developed skills and creative work to practical opportunities for income, work and entrepreneurship.

06

Sustain

Build the confidence, networks, habits and continued learning needed to keep growing beyond a single programme or opportunity.

03 / HOW WE WORK

Development, not just exposure.

The CoWiTa approach is built around the idea that meaningful development requires more than putting young people on a stage or giving them a single opportunity.

01

Start with the person

Talent development begins by understanding the young person: their ability, interests, ambitions, current skills and the direction they want to explore.

02

Learn by doing

Practical creation matters. Young people should have opportunities to apply what they learn through real creative work, projects and experiences.

03

Build with others

Mentors, peers, practitioners, communities and partners can expand what a young person can access, learn and achieve.

04

Create pathways

Development should lead somewhere. Skills need pathways into visibility, collaboration, opportunity, work and continued growth.

05

Respect creative identity

Creativity is not something to erase in order to make someone employable. The aim is to strengthen creative identity while building practical capability around it.

06

Think beyond one outcome

A successful journey should leave a young person with more than one moment of exposure. It should create knowledge, confidence, relationships and possibilities for what comes next.

04 / THE PATHWAY

The distance between talent and opportunity.

Talent can exist long before opportunity does. A young person may have ability but lack equipment, mentorship, confidence, networks, exposure, business knowledge or access to people who can open the next door.

CoWiTa is designed to work across that distance rather than treating any one intervention as the whole solution.

  • Ability becomes developed skill.
  • Skill becomes real creative work.
  • Creative work becomes visible.
  • Visibility creates connections and opportunities.
  • Opportunities can become income and sustainable pathways.
05 / WHO IT SERVES

A pathway built around people.

CoWiTa's work is intended to connect different people around the development of young talent. Each group plays a different role in the pathway.

01 / Young people

Talent & potential

Children and young people with creative interests or abilities who want to learn, develop, create and explore where their talent can take them.

02 / Mentors

Knowledge & guidance

Practitioners and experienced individuals who can share knowledge, feedback, perspective, technical skills and industry experience.

03 / Partners

Access & opportunity

Organisations, businesses, institutions and collaborators that can help create platforms, resources, networks and pathways for young talent.

07 / WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

A system around the talent.

The long-term ambition is bigger than individual workshops, performances or training sessions.

CoWiTa is building toward a connected ecosystem where a young person's talent can lead to development, practical experience, networks, platforms and sustainable opportunity.

Learning Skills and knowledge that make talent stronger.
Mentorship Guidance from people with relevant experience.
Platforms Places where creative work can be seen and experienced.
Opportunity Pathways toward collaboration, work and income.
