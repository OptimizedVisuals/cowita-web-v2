LET'S CONNECT

Good work often begins with a simple conversation.

CoWiTa brings together young people, mentors, creatives, educators, organisations, partners and communities around one shared idea: potential should have somewhere to go.

If you are wondering whether CoWiTa is the right place for your idea, talent or organisation, reach out. We can start there.
