Partnership support

Stronger youth development takes more than one organisation.

CoWiTa's work sits at the intersection of talent, creativity, skills, mentorship and opportunity. Each of those areas connects to people and organisations with different strengths.

A school may have access to young people. A creative organisation may have expertise. A business may have resources or professional networks. A technology organisation may have tools. A development organisation may have experience and reach.

Partnership support is about bringing those strengths together around a shared purpose.

What partnership can look like

Not every partnership has to look the same.

We are interested in relationships that make sense for both sides and create something useful for the young people and communities CoWiTa exists to serve.

01

Programme Support

Support the development or delivery of programmes, learning experiences, creative activities and mentorship opportunities.

02

Resource Support

Provide equipment, technology, spaces, materials or other resources that can make creative and learning opportunities more accessible.

03

Expertise & Skills

Bring professional knowledge, training, mentorship or technical expertise that can strengthen what young people and the CoWiTa team are able to do.

04

Access & Opportunity

Open doors to platforms, networks, experiences, audiences, internships, creative opportunities or other pathways for developing talent.

05

Financial Partnership

Provide financial resources that help programmes, initiatives or wider organisational development move from ideas into sustainable work.

06

Long-Term Partnership

Build an ongoing relationship around shared goals, combining resources and capabilities over time rather than supporting only one activity.

Who can partner

If you have something that can strengthen the journey, there may be a place for you here.

We are open to partnerships across sectors where there is genuine alignment with CoWiTa's purpose and a clear benefit to the young people and communities involved.

01

Schools & Learning Institutions

Create pathways for students to discover, develop and practically explore their abilities.

02

Businesses

Contribute resources, professional expertise, opportunities, technology or funding toward youth development.

03

Creative Organisations

Bring creative expertise, platforms, production capacity, mentorship or opportunities for emerging talent.

04

Community Organisations

Connect CoWiTa with communities, local knowledge, networks and existing youth-focused initiatives.

05

Development Organisations

Explore programme, capacity-building, funding or knowledge partnerships around youth development and opportunity.

06

Technology & Innovation Partners

Help build tools, platforms and digital pathways that make learning, creativity and opportunity more accessible.

01

02

Networks

Relationships and connections that can create new pathways and opportunities.

03

Platforms

04

Resources

05

Opportunity

What CoWiTa brings

Partnership should work both ways.

A meaningful partnership is not simply about what CoWiTa can receive. It should also create a clear contribution from our side. As CoWiTa develops, our role is to bring the talent-development perspective, community context and coordination needed to connect resources with meaningful youth opportunities.

A Talent-First Lens

We begin with what young people already carry and look for ways to help those abilities develop into practical pathways.

Creative Development

Creative disciplines provide a practical entry point for discovering confidence, discipline, expression and capability.

Youth-Centred Thinking

Young people should not simply be recipients of programmes. Their experiences and perspectives matter to how opportunities are designed.

Community Connection

CoWiTa is intended to connect individual talent with the wider communities, networks and opportunities around it.

A Platform for Growth

The longer-term ambition is to connect development with platforms, visibility and opportunities beyond training alone.

A Shared Mission

The strongest partnerships will be built around a genuine shared interest in expanding opportunity for young people.

How we approach partnership

Start with a conversation, not a proposal template.

We would rather understand what you care about, what you can contribute and what you hope to achieve before deciding what a partnership should look like.

01

Connect

Tell us about your organisation, your interests and why CoWiTa caught your attention.

02

Explore

We look at where your strengths and CoWiTa's work genuinely intersect.

03

Shape

Together we define the purpose, contribution, responsibilities and intended outcomes.

04

Build

We establish the practical arrangements needed to move from an idea into action.

05

Learn

We review what is working, listen to the people involved and improve the partnership as it develops.

Responsible partnership

The people we serve come first.

Because CoWiTa works around children and young people, partnerships must be built with care. Shared ambition is important, but so are safety, respect, clear responsibilities and appropriate boundaries.

Youth-centred Partnerships should ultimately create meaningful value for the young people involved.
Safe Activities involving children and young people should follow appropriate safeguarding and consent practices.
Clear Roles, responsibilities, expectations and resources should be understood before joint work begins.
Respectful Young people, communities and partner organisations should be treated with dignity throughout the relationship.
Building the partnership network

We are opening the door to the right conversations.

CoWiTa is still developing its partnership structures and programme portfolio. That means there is room for early partners to help shape how this ecosystem grows. If you see a genuine connection between your organisation and CoWiTa's purpose, we would like to hear from you.
