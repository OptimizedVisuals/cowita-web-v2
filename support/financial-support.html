I Have Talent
Financial Support

Give talent room to grow.

If you believe that a young person's talent deserves the chance to become something more, you can help make that possibility real.

A contribution to the journey

You are not funding a moment. You are helping build a pathway.

CoWiTa exists because talent alone is not always enough. Young people also need access to learning, mentorship, creative spaces, platforms, tools and opportunities to put their abilities into practice.

Financial support helps us create and strengthen those conditions. It gives CoWiTa room to develop programmes, support creative work and build the systems that allow talent to move toward opportunity.

There is no pressure to give a particular amount. Give what feels right to you, when it feels right, and know that your support is part of building something designed to last.

Your contribution

Choose how you would like to support the work.

Every supporter comes to CoWiTa with a different capacity. Some may give once. Others may prefer to contribute regularly. Both matter.

Give freely. The amount you choose should reflect what you are comfortable contributing. There is no minimum contribution displayed here because the value of your support is not defined by a single number.

Why people give

Because potential deserves somewhere to go.

Supporting CoWiTa is an opportunity to contribute to a different kind of development journey: one that starts with what a young person already carries and works toward what that talent could become.

01

Invest in potential

Help create the conditions where ability can be discovered, developed and taken seriously.

02

Create access

Help reduce the gap between having talent and having access to the experiences and resources needed to develop it.

03

Help build what lasts

Support the systems, relationships and platforms that can continue creating opportunities beyond one activity.

Where support can go

Building the things talent needs.

Financial support can strengthen different parts of CoWiTa's development pathway. As the organisation grows, we will continue to communicate clearly about how resources are being used.

Creative Development

Supporting opportunities for young people to practise, experiment and develop their creative abilities across dance, music, drawing, writing and acting.

Skills Development

Helping connect creative interests with practical skills that can strengthen confidence, capability and future opportunities.

Mentorship

Supporting the relationships and experiences through which young people can learn from people with knowledge, experience and perspective.

Platforms & Opportunities

Helping create spaces where young people can share their work, connect with others and encounter opportunities to take their next step.

Tools & Resources

Supporting access to the practical resources required to learn, create, practise and participate meaningfully.

Building CoWiTa

Helping develop the systems, coordination and infrastructure required for the work to grow responsibly and sustainably.
